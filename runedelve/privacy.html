Rune Delve – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Rune Delve ("the app") is developed by Paul Soubigou. This policy explains what data is processed when you play.

Data we collect

We do not ask for an account, your name, email or contacts. Game progress (scores, levels, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the IDFA, only if you allow tracking), approximate location from your IP address, and usage and diagnostic data to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Tracking

Personalized ads use the IDFA only if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
