Shove Off! – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Shove Off! ("the app") is developed by Paul Soubigou. This policy explains what data is processed when you play.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in and no tracking. Game progress (levels, settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising and purchases

The app shows no ads and has no in-app purchases.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. If this changes in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated first.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
